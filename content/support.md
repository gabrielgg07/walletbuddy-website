## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I connect a financial account?

Sign in to Wallet Buddy and use the financial account connection feature.

Wallet Buddy uses Plaid to securely facilitate connections with supported financial institutions.

### Why isn't my account balance updating?

Wallet Buddy currently synchronizes financial information nightly.

Balances and transactions may not reflect the most recent activity at your financial institution.

Some connections may require renewed authorization.

### How do I disconnect a bank account?

Open Wallet Buddy and navigate to the connected-account management feature.

Select the institution or account you want to disconnect and use the available disconnect option.

### How do I delete my Wallet Buddy account?

Use the account-deletion option within Wallet Buddy.

Account deletion initiates removal of associated application data according to the Privacy Policy.

If you encounter problems, contact gabegonzal2107@gmail.com.

### Does Wallet Buddy cost money?

No. Wallet Buddy is currently free, with no subscriptions or in-app purchases.

### Can Wallet Buddy move or withdraw my money?

No. Wallet Buddy currently uses read-only financial account connections and does not support payments, transfers, or investment trades.

### Is Wallet Buddy affiliated with my bank?

Wallet Buddy is an independent application and is not endorsed by connected financial institutions.
