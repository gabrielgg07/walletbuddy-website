## 1. Introduction

Wallet Buddy ("Wallet Buddy," "we," "us," or "our") is a personal financial management application developed by Gabriel Gnzalez.

Wallet Buddy helps users view connected financial accounts, monitor transactions, categorize spending, and track their net worth.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and delete personal information when users access Wallet Buddy.

Wallet Buddy is currently intended for a limited group of users, with potential public distribution in the United States in the future.

## 2. Information We Collect

### Account Information

When users register or sign in through Supabase Authentication, we process information necessary to create and maintain their accounts, including:

- Email addresses and authentication identifiers
- User account identifiers
- Authentication and session information

### Financial Information

When users voluntarily connect financial accounts using Plaid, Wallet Buddy may access and store:

- Financial institution names
- Financial account names, types, and identifiers
- Bank account balances
- Credit card balances
- Transaction dates, amounts, descriptions, and categories
- Debt and loan balances and related information made available through authorized connections

Users may also manually enter financial balances.

The financial information collected depends on the accounts connected and the permissions granted.

### Technical Information

Our backend and infrastructure providers may process technical information necessary to operate and secure the application, such as API request metadata, authentication events, IP addresses, and service logs.

Wallet Buddy does not currently integrate separate advertising, product analytics, or third-party crash-reporting services.

## 3. Financial Account Connections

Wallet Buddy uses Plaid to connect supported financial institutions.

When a user connects an account, Plaid facilitates the authorization process and provides permitted financial information to Wallet Buddy.

Wallet Buddy stores Plaid access tokens in encrypted form in its Supabase database.

Wallet Buddy does not directly request users' online banking passwords through its own interface.

Plaid processes information under its own privacy practices:

https://plaid.com/legal/

Users may disconnect their linked financial accounts through Wallet Buddy.

Disconnecting an account stops Wallet Buddy's authorized synchronization for that connection, subject to successful revocation or removal of the corresponding provider connection.

Previously synchronized information may remain until it is deleted according to the application's data-deletion procedures.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use personal and financial information to:

- Authenticate users and maintain accounts
- Retrieve and display authorized financial account information
- Track account balances and net worth
- Display transaction histories
- Categorize spending
- Calculate financial summaries and trends
- Synchronize financial information through scheduled background processes
- Maintain application security and functionality
- Respond to support and privacy requests

Wallet Buddy currently synchronizes connected financial information through a nightly scheduled process.

Wallet Buddy does not currently use personal financial information for advertising or AI-powered analysis.

## 5. Data Storage and Security

Wallet Buddy stores its application database, including financial account balances and transaction histories, using Supabase.

Wallet Buddy's backend uses HTTPS for API communications and requires authentication for protected API operations.

The application uses Supabase Row Level Security to restrict database access according to configured authorization policies.

Plaid access tokens are stored in encrypted form.

Wallet Buddy does not intentionally persist financial account balances or transaction histories in a local application database on users' devices.

Temporary processing or caching by the operating system, networking components, or service providers may still occur.

We use security measures intended to protect personal information; however, no electronic system can guarantee complete security.

## 6. Service Providers and Data Sharing

Wallet Buddy uses the following service providers to operate its functionality:

**Supabase**
- User authentication
- Database storage
- Backend data access controls

**Plaid**
- Financial institution connectivity
- Authorized retrieval of financial information

**Vercel**
- Hosting and execution of Wallet Buddy's backend services

We share or make information accessible to these providers as necessary to operate Wallet Buddy.

Wallet Buddy does not currently sell personal information, display advertisements, or intentionally share users' financial information with data brokers or advertising networks.

We may disclose information if required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

## 7. Data Retention

Wallet Buddy retains user account information and stored financial information while the user's account remains active, unless earlier deletion is requested or performed.

Users may delete their accounts using the application's account-deletion functionality.

Account deletion is intended to remove the user's application account and associated financial records, including stored balances, transactions, and connected-account credentials.

Deletion from operational databases may not immediately remove information from infrastructure backups, provider systems, or records that must be retained under applicable law.

Wallet Buddy is intended exclusively for individuals aged 18 or older.

The application is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under 18.

If we become aware that an underage individual has registered, we will take appropriate steps to address the account and associated information.

## 10. Financial Information Disclaimer

Wallet Buddy is an informational financial management application.

Financial account information may be delayed, incomplete, or inaccurate due to synchronization schedules, third-party provider availability, or other technical limitations.

Users should verify important information directly with their financial institutions.

Wallet Buddy does not currently support initiating payments, transferring funds, or executing investment trades.
